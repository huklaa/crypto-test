Why this NFT?

Chainling is a digital souvenir for early Robinhood Chain explorers. Every design has a fixed edition cap, making each release easy to understand and independently verifiable on-chain.

Transparent by design

Mint price, edition limits and the verified collection contract will be published before paid minting opens. Transactions and ownership remain publicly auditable on Robinhood Chain.

The journey may continue

Chainling is a fan-made collectible, not an investment product or an official Robinhood Markets release. No future benefit is guaranteed, but holders may be considered for community perks, special experiences, airdrops, allowlists or other surprises as Chainling evolves.

Collection identity

Seven visual editions share one Early Explorer theme. Each design has its own supply cap while remaining part of the same Chainling collection story.

Ownership first

The collectible is designed to live in the holder's wallet as an on-chain record. Once minting is live, ownership and edition balances can be checked independently through the explorer.

Simple mint economics

The intended public mint price is 0.50 USDG per NFT. No hidden interface fee is planned; any wallet-displayed network cost is separate from the collection price.
